1. Move the “changes apply to new payouts only” note next to Save

Venue SettingsOshada (Venue Settings feedback, item 1)

Issue in the first build

The note sat in a paragraph under the heading, a full screen away from Save. Someone pressing Save would not have it in view, so the effect of saving was easy to miss.

Feedback

Move the “changes apply to new payouts only” note near the Save button so users understand the impact before saving.

First build

After the feedback

Workflow after the feedback

  1. Open Manage, then Venues, and select a venue.
  2. Change any setting, for example turn Cheque on.
  3. Look at the buttons at the bottom: “Changes apply to new payouts only. Submitted payouts are not affected.” sits directly above Cancel and Save.
  4. Select Save. A confirmation message appears, and reloading the page shows the saved values.

Open live: Venue Settings (Super Admin view)

Changed in: VenueSettingsForm

2. Toggles: clear label, state announced, keyboard, state shown in text

Venue SettingsOshada (Venue Settings feedback, item 2)

Issue in the first build

The switch was already a real switch control with a label and a visible focus ring, but its state was shown only by colour (blue or grey). Nothing said On or Off in words, and the explanation under each title was not linked to the switch for screen readers.

Feedback

Ensure each toggle has a clear label, announces its on/off state, and works with a keyboard. Show the state in text where it helps users understand the impact.

First build

After the feedback

Workflow after the feedback

  1. Open a venue. Each switch now has the word On or Off beside it.
  2. Press Tab to reach a switch. A focus ring appears.
  3. Press Space or Enter to switch it. The word and the colour change together.
  4. A screen reader reads the method name, On or Off, and the line of description under the title.
  5. Turning Payment File for Your Bank on reveals Venue Code. Turning Cheque on reveals the question about who can enter cheque details. Turning either off hides its extra settings.

Open live: Venue Settings (Super Admin view)

Changed in: Switch, PaymentMethodRow

3. Shorter descriptions, detail only when needed

Venue SettingsOshada (Venue Settings feedback, item 3) and Akila

Issue in the first build

Every card carried a full sentence or two of explanation, the page opened with a paragraph before any control, and the cheque question and its three options were long. The page took a lot of reading for a screen with three settings.

Feedback

Reduce reading time with brief descriptions and reveal extra detail only when needed. For example: Funds transfer — Always available; Bank payment file — On/off; Cheque — On/off. Keep the venue-code note (“Included in the file; leading zeros are kept”) and shorten the cheque question to Who can enter cheque details? (Akila: try to make it less wordy if possible, no need to mention everything, too much text here.)

First build

After the feedback

Workflow after the feedback

  1. Open a venue. The intro paragraph under “How Winners Are Paid” is gone.
  2. Each method has one short line: Funds Transfer “Paid into the winner’s bank account automatically.”, Payment File for Your Bank “Your team uploads a payment file to your bank’s online portal.”, Cheque “Winners are paid by cheque.”
  3. Turn Payment File for Your Bank on. Venue Code appears with “Included in the file; leading zeros are kept.”
  4. Turn Cheque on. The question reads “Who can enter cheque details?” with three one-line options: Collector Only, Authoriser Only, Collector and Authoriser Both.
  5. Turn them off again and the extra settings disappear.

Open live: Venue Settings (Super Admin view)

Changed in: VenueSettingsForm, venue-data (option text), ChequeModeOptions

4. Funds Transfer looks like something you can switch

Venue SettingsOshada (Venue Settings feedback, item 4, optional)

Issue in the first build

Funds Transfer used the same white card as the two methods that have switches, with no switch. A reader could think it was missing a control, or that it could be turned off.

Feedback

Funds Transfer is always available, but its card looks configurable. Label it Always available or style it differently.

First build

After the feedback

Workflow after the feedback

  1. Open a venue. Funds Transfer is a lighter, outlined card.
  2. It has the label “Always available” on the right, and no switch.
  3. The two cards below it, Payment File for Your Bank and Cheque, keep their switches.

Open live: Venue Settings (Super Admin view)

Changed in: AlwaysAvailableMethodRow (new)

5. “Cheque Details Entered By” shows the actual username

Approver and Authoriser review (Payment Method Details card)Oshada (Payout Summary)

Issue in the first build

The row showed the venue setting (for example “Collector and Authoriser Both”). That says who is allowed to enter the details, not who did, so it could not tell a reviewer who to ask about a cheque.

Feedback

Cheque Details Entered By: Should be populated by the actual username other than the available options.

After the feedback

6. Details the Authoriser enters fill the card, no new placeholders

Authoriser review (Payment Method Details card)Oshada (Authoriser entry)

Issue in the first build

The card first listed “Not entered yet” for the cheque number and name, with a separate form underneath. After the Authoriser checked the details, the values appeared in a second block, so the same two labels were on screen twice and the first pair still said Not entered yet.

Feedback

When the cheque details are entered by Authoriser, the details should be populated in the card instead of showing new placeholders.

First build

After the feedback

Workflow after the feedback

  1. Open the Authoriser review with the Cheque: Authoriser Only payout.
  2. Under Payment Method Details, Cheque Details Entered By says Not entered yet, and the Cheque Number and Cheque Name fields sit inside the card. There is no separate form.
  3. Enter both and select Validate Cheque. Leaving one empty shows a message under that field.
  4. The values now fill the card (Cheque Number, Cheque Name) with “Cheque bearer name matches.” and an edit pencil. Cheque Details Entered By shows R.Nguyen (Authoriser). The second screenshot is the Collector and Authoriser payout, where the card lists both people.
  5. Tick the confirmation box. Authorise is now available. Selecting the pencil reopens the fields and switches Authorise off until the details are checked again.

Open live: Authoriser review, Cheque: Authoriser Only

Changed in: ChequeDetailsEditor, RecordedDestination, AuthoriserPayoutView

7. A switched-off method sends the Collector straight to Payment Breakdown

Collector flow (MBT-10, CHE-07)Oshada (on MBT-10)

Issue in the first build

When the venue turned off the method the Collector had chosen, the Collector still opened Payment Method Details, saw a warning there and a disabled Next, and had to find their own way back to choose again. The screen they landed on was one they could not use.

Feedback

Instead of opening the Payment Method Details page, showing the warning and taking the collector directly to the Payment Breakdown would be ideal.

First build

After the feedback

Workflow after the feedback

  1. A Collector chooses a way to pay the rest on Payment Breakdown, for example Payment File for Your Bank, and continues through the steps.
  2. Meanwhile an Admin switches that method off for the venue (Manage, Venues, turn it off, Save).
  3. The Collector reaches the Payment Method Details step.
  4. They are sent straight back to Payment Breakdown. A warning at the top says “Payment File for Your Bank is no longer available at this venue. Choose another way to pay the rest.”
  5. How the Rest Is Paid lists only the methods still available. The earlier choice is cleared. In this prototype the amounts typed on Payment Breakdown are not kept.

Open live: Collector, Payment Breakdown (switch the method off at the venue first)

Changed in: PayoutDestinationDetailsForm, PaymentBreakdownForm, collector-data
